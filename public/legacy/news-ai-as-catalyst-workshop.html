Location Guangzhou, China
Format Full-day workshop and panel

AI as Catalyst is a full-day workshop taking place on May 12, 2026 at HKUST(GZ), bringing together students, educators, clinicians, and researchers to examine how artificial intelligence should be designed, evaluated, and governed in healthcare and education.

The program combines hands-on experimentation, interdisciplinary discussion, and collaborative design. Rather than treating AI as a shortcut for efficiency, the workshop is framed around critical engagement: participants will test capabilities, examine failure modes, and discuss the social and institutional safeguards needed for responsible deployment.

Three Hands-on Tracks

The Art of Healing: Creative Arts as Pedagogical Practice

Experience creativity as a core way of knowing required for healing and explore how it can be embedded into medical education.

This track treats artistic practice not as an add-on to medical education, but as a way of knowing essential to healing.

Health AI Systems Thinking for Community (HASTC)

This track encourages cross-disciplinary discussions and collaborative analyses of recent case studies on potential problems originating from clinical AI.

It emphasizes systems thinking across models, workflows, institutions, and communities rather than treating failures as isolated technical issues.

LLM-a-thon: From Use Case to Ground Truth

Experiment with AI prompts and test the power of LLMs in medical scenarios.

Participants will move from concrete use cases to ground-truth checking in order to understand both the promise and the limits of model outputs in practice.

Venue

The workshop will be held at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), 1 Duxue Road, Nansha District, Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

By bringing healthcare, education, HCI, and AI governance into the same conversation, AI as Catalyst positions the campus as a space for practical experimentation, critical reflection, and cross-disciplinary collaboration around responsible AI.
