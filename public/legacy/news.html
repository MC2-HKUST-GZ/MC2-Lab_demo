AI as Catalyst Workshop to Explore AI in Healthcare and Education at HKUST(GZ)

Published: 10 Apr 2026

Scheduled for May 12, 2026 in Guangzhou, this full-day workshop brings together students, educators, clinicians, and researchers to examine AI through hands-on experimentation, critical discussion, and collaborative design.

The program includes LLM-a-thon, Health AI Systems Thinking for Community, a panel session, and a closing workshop on technology's role in healthcare and art education, with invited speakers from HKUST(GZ), MIT, Harvard, NUS, HKU, CUHK, and Emory.

Japan Times Highlights MC2 Lab Research on Human-AI Romantic Attachment

Published: 13 Feb 2026

In its feature on AI dating and marriage in Japan, The Japan Times cites research by Xuetong Wang, Ching Christie Pang, and Pan Hui on emotional attachment to AI companions.

The cited mixed-methods study (\"My Dataset of Love\") suggests that forming genuine feelings toward virtual entities reflects a broader cognitive tendency rather than an isolated anomaly.

Source: The Japan Times | Paper: ACM DOI 10.1145/3757532

MC2 Nature Spotlight: HKUST(GZ) in the Global Conversation on AI-Native Education

Published: 26 Dec 2025

Nature's feature report on the future of higher education highlights HKUST(GZ) Professor Pan Hui's team and its classroom deployment of digital avatar teachers powered by large language models.

The story discusses both opportunities and risks of AI-first universities, including personalized learning, academic ethics, critical thinking, and privacy.

Source: HKUST(GZ) WeChat article
